About this system

The Enhanced OM System (internally the "Sales Comment Manager") is a full-stack tool that reconciles rows from the production-order-tracking table against the scheduling team's backlog workbook by Sales Doc. and Item. It gives planners one place to review matched orders and capture the OM commentary that used to live only in a scattered spreadsheet, so the whole team can see the current status and history for every open sales order at a glance.

  • Backlog matching: joins live order rows to the backlog seed sheet by Sales Document and Item number.
  • Per-order editor: a modal captures Category, OM Comments, Procurement Feedback, and Traffic Comments for each row.
  • OM Period preview & batch update: lets planners validate a batch of orders before writing a new OM Period value.
  • Category tracking: On Track / At Risk / Escalated / Hold chips give a fast visual read of order health.
  • Search & filter: quickly narrow the grid by document number, material, or customer while triaging a backlog.
