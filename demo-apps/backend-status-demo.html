    About this system

    This demo previews the core screen of Trench Canada Coils' internal Backlog & OM Period Status Board — a scheduling tool used on the shop floor and by planning staff to track open sales orders as they move through each manufacturing workcenter. All data on this page is fabricated sample content for demonstration purposes only; the real application connects to live production and MES systems and is not reachable from this page.

    • Per-workcenter "Pending" tables for STRIP, CRIMP, WELD2, BLAST, CLEAN, PAINT, TESTLAB, DETAIL and CRATE, each showing open sales orders queued at that station.
    • A live shop-floor order confirmation feed that refreshes roughly every 30 seconds as operators confirm completed operations.
    • A Material Check table cross-referenced against pending orders, flagging shortages that could delay a workcenter.
    • A CRATED tab listing orders that are packed and ready to ship, plus a History tab for orders already shipped.
    • An admin-only Overdue Orders tab, and a "Why" notes/comments feature so planners can annotate the reason behind a delay on any order.
