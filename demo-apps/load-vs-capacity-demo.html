About this system

The Load vs Capacity Platform gives Sales and Production a shared, high-level picture of whether upcoming order volume fits within available coil-line capacity — without needing to dig into the detailed shop-floor schedule. It's designed as a conversation tool: quick to read in a meeting, quick to re-run with a "what if" scenario.

  • Monthly load-vs-capacity visualization across a rolling multi-month window
  • Load ratio trendline to spot months trending toward or past the ceiling
  • Lightweight simulation overlay for "additional load %" what-if scenarios
  • Built for Sales ↔ Production capacity discussions, not detailed line scheduling
  • Companion views for schedules, SAP orders, shift workload and vacation capacities
