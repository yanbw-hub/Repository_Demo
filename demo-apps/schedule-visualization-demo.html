About this system

The real TCC Scheduling & Visualization Integrated Platform is the production planning tool used by Winding & Roving planners to sequence orders across WIND1–WIND5, check machine loading, and stress-test the schedule before committing changes back to SAP. It combines SAP exports (characteristics, hours, lead orders, production orders, sales orders, and calendars) with an editable Gantt-style schedule table, then recalculates finish dates through a scheduling engine so planners can preview and download an updated schedule workbook. This preview shows only the visual style and core Gantt/simulation interaction of the Prod Orders Schedule screen, running entirely on invented sample data.

  • Dashboard — control-tower home page summarizing backlog, load vs capacity, reworks and vacation status.
  • Backlog Summary — SAP orders, TCC backlog and vacation tables joined into month/line drill-downs with CSV export.
  • Prod Orders Schedule — the Gantt view shown here: L1/L2/VPI schedule types, line filters, and per-order hover detail.
  • Simulation & Machine Load Simulation — capacity-driven what-if runs that project finish dates and line loading under different assumptions.
  • Reworks, Vacation Management & Load vs Capacity — supporting views used to communicate risk and impact to management.
